## Original failure, reviewed recovery and retained evidence

The original execution completed 47 strictly accepted tasks (235 episodes). Its first `WaffleReheat` native invocation also finished with exit code 0, no timeout, five episodes, 640 steps and zero successes. Its guardian nevertheless remained **failed**: global scoped cleanup could not read `/proc/43324/environ` (PermissionDenied), while its remaining-owned-process list and final GPU compute list were empty.

PID 43324 had already disappeared during later diagnosis. No contemporaneous PID/start/parent/namespace evidence established ownership. Its classification remains **unclassified**; the report does not call it unrelated or retrospectively upgrade that failed audit.

Root explicitly reviewed one infrastructure retry. The external continuation reused the first 47 tasks without rerunning them, selected `WaffleReheat/attempt-2`, then ran `WashFruitColander/attempt-1` and `WeighIngredients/attempt-1`. It retained the original failed outcome, original incomplete summary, progress/running snapshots, and the five unselected first-attempt records. No score-based retry or score-based choice occurred.
